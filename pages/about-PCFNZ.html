About Prostate Cancer Foundation of New Zealand

Prostate Cancer Foundation of New Zealand – Te Kāhui Mate Pukupuku Repe Tātea o Aotearoa Manatōpu (PCFNZ), operates two customer facing brands: Prostate Cancer Foundation NZ and Testicular Cancer NZ – Mate Pukupuku Raho Aotearoa (TCNZ), reflecting a whole-of-life approach to men’s health.

Not only providing vital support, education and information to patients, their families and whānau, we strive through both brands to reduce the impact prostate and testicular cancer by raising awareness, funding NZ-based research and advocating for improved standards of care.

PCFNZ pursues a world where those affected by either cancer can navigate their health with knowledge, confidence and dignity. PCFNZ is there for them when they need it the most, with a nurse-led information service; round-the-clock counselling; LGBTQIA+ Rainbow Blue peer support and national network offering holistic support and fellowship.
